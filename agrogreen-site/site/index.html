Questions

What buyers ask first.

Which months can you supply?

Sweet potato ships from 1 July to 1 March. Dry onions ship from 1 May to the end of July. Green beans ship from 1 September to the end of March.

Do you grow organic?

Yes. Our sweet potato is grown both organic and conventional, planted from tissue culture.

How is the product kept on the way?

Product is held at 14°C or below through the supply chain, in temperature controlled packing centres and insulated packaging. We ship by air and by sea.

What sizes and pack formats do you offer?

Send us your spec in the quote form. We reply with the sizes, pack formats and volumes we can meet for your delivery window.

Where are you based?

Our farms and packing are in Egypt. We have offices in Cairo and in London.

How long have you been exporting?

Agro Green was established in 2018. Our team has more than 20 years of experience growing, packing and exporting to the European market.
